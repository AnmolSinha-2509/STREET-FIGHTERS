Tournament Rules

👥 Team Size

Each squad must consist of 4 players only.

🎮 Game Mode

Available modes: Battle Royale (Squad) & Clash Squad. Teams must play in the mode announced for the tournament.

💰 Entry Fee

₹200 for Battle Royale (BR) & ₹100 for Clash Squad (CS).

🚫 Fair Play

No hacks or unfair practices allowed. Cheaters will be banned.

📸 Proof

Teams must submit match screenshots after every round.

⚠️ Discipline

Disrespectful behavior will lead to immediate disqualification.

🏆 Final Say

The decision of the organizers is final and binding.
